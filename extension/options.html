SpoolRSS

Shows your unread count in the toolbar. Click the icon to see your latest unread articles, open your reader, or subscribe to the feeds on the page you're viewing.

When you connect, your browser asks to allow access to that one address. The extension can't see any other site, except the page you're on when you click its toolbar button, which it checks for feeds.

Don't have a feed reader yet? SpoolRSS is free and runs on your own server.
